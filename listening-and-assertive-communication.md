# Listening and Assertive Communication - Answers

## 1. Active Listening

### Question 1
What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)
- Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts and focus entirely on the speaker and topic.
- Try not to interrupt the other person while they are speaking.
- Let the speaker finish completely before you create your response.
- Use "door openers" (lines like "Tell me more" or "Go ahead, I'm listening") to keep them talking.
- Show that you are actively listening by using engaging and positive body language.
- Take notes during important conversations and modify what the speaker said to ensure you are on the same page.



## 2. Reflective Listening

### Question 2
According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? 
- Reflective listening is an advanced form of active listening that focuses on mirroring emotions, body language, and verifying the exact message.
- It is commonly used in critical or high-stakes environments (like meetings) and to calm down emotionally charged personal conversations.



## 3. Reflection

### Question 3
What are the obstacles in your listening process?
- Getting distracted by internal thoughts, multitasking, or thinking about what to say next instead of paying attention.
- Emotional reactions, siding, or external noise that prevent fully grasping the speaker's message.

### Question 4
What can you do to improve your listening?
- Practice conscious focus by eliminating distractions and maintaining open body language.
- Actively take notes, ask clarifying questions, and paraphrase to confirm understanding before responding.



## 4. Types of Communication

### Question 5
When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?
- I tend to switch to a passive style when I want to avoid conflicts or when I am dealing with an overly aggressive person.
- I also use it when I lack confidence in my opinion or feel hesitant to speak up in large groups.

### Question 6
When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?
- I switch to an aggressive style when I feel extremely frustrated, overwhelmed, or when my boundaries are continuously crossed.
- It also happens during high-pressure situations when immediate action is needed, but emotions take over.

### Question 7
When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?
- I resort to passive-aggressive behavior when I am upset or annoyed with someone but hesitant to address the issue directly.
- It often happens when holding onto hidden resentment rather than communicating boundaries clearly.

### Question 8
How can you make your communication assertive? You can watch and analyse the videos, then think what would be a few steps you can apply in your own life? 
- Clearly express my thoughts, feelings, and needs using "I" statements without blaming others.
- Maintain a calm tone of voice and open body language while respecting the opinions and boundaries of the other person.
