# Prevention of Sexual Harassment



### 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment happens when someone says, does, or shows things that make another person feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or disrespected at work based on sex or gender. This includes:
* **Unwanted physical touch:** Touching, grabbing, or coming too close on purpose.
* **Bad remarks or jokes:** Passing sexual comments, making vulgar jokes, or teasing someone about their body or personal life.
* **Inappropriate messages or pictures:** Sending unwanted texts, emails, or showing offensive pictures, posters, or videos.
* **Asking for favors:** Demanding dates or personal favors in exchange for keeping a job, getting a promotion, or receiving good grades/evaluations.
* **Staring or gestures:** Making rude hand gestures or staring at someone in a way that makes them feel threatened.

### 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face or see anyone else facing this kind of behavior, I would take the following steps:
* **Speak up clearly:** If it feels safe, I would tell the person right away to stop because their behavior is not okay and makes me (or the other person) uncomfortable.
* **Keep a record:** I would note down what happened, including the date, time, what was said or done, and any witnesses. I would save copies of any offensive messages or emails.
* **Report it:** I would immediately report the matter to higher authorities, HR, or the company's Internal team.
* **Support the victim:** If I am a witness, I would step in to help if possible, stand by the person who faced it, and offer to back up their story when they report it.
