# Learning Process


## How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

**1. What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**\
**Answer:**\
    The technique is all about four steps: 1. Write down the concept name; 2. Explain the concept to someone in your own language so others can understand; 3. Practice as many examples as possible; 4. Identify the areas you are not able to explain clearly and start again

**3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**\
**Answer:**\
    Active mode is like when you are directly studying, working through specific practice problems, or applying known rules and formulas. Diffuse Mode is like A relaxed, resting state of neural activity that allows your brain to view things from a much broader, macro perspective.

## Learn Anything in 20 hours

**4. According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**\
**Answer:**\
      The steps to take when approaching is:
      -Deconstruct the skill into smaller, manageable sub-skills and practice the most important parts first.
      -Learn just enough to self-correct by gathering a few resources without falling into procrastination.
      -Remove barriers to practice and commit to at least 20 hours of focused effort to overcome the initial frustration.
