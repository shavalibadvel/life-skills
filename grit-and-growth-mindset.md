# Grit and Growth Mindset 

### Question 1
> *Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.*

**Answer:** 
Grit is basically about not giving up when things get hard. It means sticking to your long-term goals and working through failures instead of quitting.

### Question 2
> *Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.*

**Answer:** 
A growth mindset means believing you can get better at things through hard work and practice. Instead of thinking intelligence or talent is fixed, you see challenges as a way to learn.

---

### Question 3
> *What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?*

**Answer:** 
Having an internal locus of control means believing that you are responsible for your own actions, choices, and success rather than blaming outside luck or circumstances. The main point is that taking personal responsibility gives you the power to change your life because you stop waiting for things to happen to you and start making them happen.
